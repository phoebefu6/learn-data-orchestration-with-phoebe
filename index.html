Daybreak's nightly build runs on cron: thirty mornings out of thirty on time, twelve of them quietly wrong. This course upgrades that pipeline the honest way - dependencies that wait for facts, retries that know their price, tasks you can rerun without fear, a backfill that repairs history, and a page that fires while there is still night left. Every rung is measured across the same thirty nights, including the one lever that never helps.
